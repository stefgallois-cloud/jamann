## Prompt

```
Animate this exact character from the reference image — keep its design,
colors, proportions and face 100% identical, do not redesign it.

Scene: the robot character is working at a small cluttered workshop desk,
in its own cozy "atelier". It's tinkering with a glowing floating gear
between its clawed hands, turning it slowly with focused curiosity. Behind
it, soft blurred workshop shapes: a shelf with tools, a small screen with
abstract glowing UI panels, a warm desk lamp casting soft light.

Camera: slow gentle push-in, static tripod-style shot, no shake, cinematic
but subtle.

Motion: the robot's antenna tip pulses softly, its eyes blink once, its
head tilts slightly as it inspects the gear, then it looks up toward
camera and smiles.

Lighting: warm key light from the desk lamp mixing with soft ambient glow
in indigo #6366F1 and cyan #06B6D4 tones, soft rim light on the character
edges. Background stays darker (#0F172A tones) so the character pops.

Style: same flat-illustration-with-soft-3D toy-like rendering as the
reference image, smooth and clean motion, no photorealism, no rough
sketchy animation.

Duration: 8 seconds, loopable feel, calm pacing.
Audio: soft, minimal ambient workshop sounds (gentle mechanical clicks,
faint hum), no dialogue, no music with lyrics.

--no redesign of the character, no extra characters or people, no
readable text or logos appearing, no camera shake, no glitch effects,
no watermark
```

**Usage :** template réutilisable — seule la partie "Scene" change pour d'autres mises en situation (répond au téléphone, colle un post-it, etc.).
