## Prompt

```
Animate this exact character from the reference image — keep its design,
colors, proportions and face 100% identical, do not redesign it. This is
a high-energy cinematic action shot, not a simple loop. Strong 3D camera
movement, big "wow" factor.

CONCEPT: The robot is surfing on a fast-flowing river of glowing light —
a visual representation of time itself as a rushing current. It is in
full control, riding it with confidence and joy, until it reaches out
and catches a single particle of time, freezing the entire world around
it in a dramatic slow-motion finale.

SETTING: A vast, dark 3D void (#0F172A) that curves and stretches into
the distance like a canyon made of light. Below and around the robot,
a wide luminous river of streaming particles flows fast — a mix of
indigo #6366F1 and violet #A78BFA light streaks, with glowing translucent
clock faces, gear shapes and hourglass silhouettes embedded in the flow,
rushing past like debris in rapids. Cyan #06B6D4 sparks trail off the
edges of the current.

NARRATIVE ARC (0-10s):
0.0-1.0s — Fast establishing shot: camera swoops down from above into
the light-river canyon, diving toward the robot who is already riding
the current, standing balanced and confident on a surfboard-like plate
of solid light, knees slightly bent, arms out for balance, big excited
smile.

1.0-4.5s — Dynamic lateral tracking shot, camera flying alongside the
robot at high speed, matching its motion exactly (like a chase-cam in an
action film). The river banks rush past in motion-blurred streaks of
indigo/violet light. Glowing clock and gear silhouettes blur past on
both sides. The robot leans into a few gentle carving turns on the wave
of light, sending up sprays of cyan sparks with each turn, clearly
enjoying the speed.

4.5-6.5s — The camera whips around to a three-quarter front angle, still
matching speed, as the robot spots something ahead: a single, brighter,
slower-moving particle of light glowing warm and distinct amid the fast
stream. The robot crouches lower on its light-board, focused, reaching
one clawed hand forward.

6.5-8.0s — Dramatic slow-motion moment: everything decelerates sharply.
The robot's hand closes around the glowing particle. The instant it
grips it, a shockwave of soft cyan-to-white light ripples outward in
3D through the entire canyon.

8.0-10.0s — The whole river of rushing light freezes mid-motion — every
streak, spark, clock and gear hangs suspended in space like a photograph
of the flow, glowing softly and steadily. The robot stands upright and
still on its now-frozen light platform, calm, confident, smiling directly
at camera, holding the single glowing particle in its open hand. Camera
does a slow final arc/orbit around the robot to reveal the frozen river
extending into the distance, then settles.

CAMERA: fast, fluid, 3D chase-cam style movement for most of the shot
(dive-in, lateral tracking, whip-pan), then a hard shift into slow-motion
with a smooth final orbit — big dynamic range between speed and stillness
is the point. No shaky handheld feel — always fluid, controlled,
big-budget-film camera work.

LIGHTING: dark void background (#0F172A) throughout, all light sourced
from the river of particles and the robot's own glowing accents (chest
light, eyes, feet) in indigo #6366F1, violet #A78BFA and cyan #06B6D4.
Strong motion blur and light trails during the fast section, crisp clean
glow with soft bloom once frozen.

STYLE: the character itself keeps the flat-illustration-with-soft-3D
toy-like rendering from the reference image exactly as-is. The
environment and light effects around it are big-budget cinematic 3D —
think high-end animated action-film sequence, sleek and futuristic, not
photoreal, not cyberpunk-grungy.

MOOD: exhilarating, fast, joyful, powerful — then a satisfying, calm,
triumphant stillness at the end. The feeling of mastering time rather
than being ruled by it.

AUDIO: rushing whoosh and light chime textures building with the speed
of the ride, a subtle rising orchestral swell during the reach-and-grab,
a sharp soft "freeze" sound effect (like glass chiming) on the shockwave,
resolving into a warm, calm, low ambient tone as the camera settles. No
dialogue, no music with lyrics.

Duration: 10 seconds.

--no redesign of the character, no extra characters or people, no
readable text, numbers or logos anywhere in frame, no shaky handheld
camera, no cuts (must read as one continuous shot with camera moves, not
edited clips), no glitch/cyberpunk-grunge aesthetic, no dystopian or cold
mood, no watermark
```
