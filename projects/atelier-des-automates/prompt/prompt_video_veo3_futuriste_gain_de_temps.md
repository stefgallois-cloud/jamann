# Prompt vidéo Veo 3 — Scène cinématographique futuriste, gain de temps

Image de référence à fournir : `landing/assets/logo-robot-v2-transparent.png`
(recoloration indigo/violet/cyan validée le 2026-07-03, voir `branding.md`)

**Intention narrative :** incarner en 8 secondes le "pourquoi" du projet
(voir `storytelling.md`) — le temps perdu sur les tâches manuelles, répétitives,
qui se dissout instantanément grâce à l'IA. Pas de texte à l'écran, tout
passe par la mise en scène (horloge, lumière, vitesse).

---

## Prompt

```
Animate this exact character from the reference image — keep its design,
colors, proportions and face 100% identical, do not redesign it. This is
a short cinematic narrative, not a simple loop.

CONCEPT: A story about reclaiming time. The scene visually contrasts the
old way (frantic, manual, time draining away) with the new way (the robot
character effortlessly compressing hours of work into an instant).

SETTING: A sleek, futuristic version of a workshop — dark, minimal,
almost like a control room floating in soft space. Suspended holographic
panels made of thin glowing light lines float around, in indigo #6366F1
and violet #A78BFA. The floor and walls are near-black (#0F172A), so all
light comes from the glowing elements themselves.

NARRATIVE ARC (0-8s):
0.0-2.0s — Wide static shot. Dozens of small translucent clock faces and
hourglasses float in the dark space around the robot, their hands
spinning too fast, sand pouring out quickly — a visual metaphor for hours
being lost. The robot stands small and still in the middle, looking
slightly overwhelmed, shoulders low, watching the clocks spin around it.

2.0-4.0s — The robot raises one clawed hand and gently touches a single
floating clock face. The instant it touches it, that clock's frantic
spin freezes. A ripple of soft cyan #06B6D4 light pulses outward from
the robot's hand through the whole scene.

4.0-6.5s — Chain reaction: one by one, every other floating clock and
hourglass around the robot stops spinning and gently dissolves into
calm streaks of light (indigo to cyan gradient), which flow inward and
get absorbed into the robot's chest panel, making its chest light glow
brighter. Camera slowly orbits around the robot as this happens, low
and dynamic but smooth, no shake.

6.5-8.0s — All the light settles. The chaotic swarm of clocks is gone,
replaced by a single, large, calm holographic clock face floating gently
in front of the robot, its hands moving slowly and steadily now. The
robot lowers its hand, looks up at the calm clock, and smiles — posture
relaxed, shoulders up, confident. Soft indigo/cyan glow bathes the scene.
Camera settles into a final steady medium shot.

CAMERA: cinematic, slow deliberate movements only (static → slow orbit →
settle), shallow depth of field on background elements, no shake, no
handheld feel, no fast cuts — this must read as one continuous shot.

LIGHTING: mostly dark, moody, near-black background (#0F172A), all light
sourced from glowing UI/clock elements and the robot's own chest light,
in indigo #6366F1, violet #A78BFA and cyan #06B6D4 tones. High contrast,
soft glow/bloom on light sources, no harsh white light.

STYLE: same flat-illustration-with-soft-3D toy-like character rendering
as the reference image, but the environment around it is more
photoreal-adjacent sci-fi/holographic (glass-like translucent panels,
soft particle light) — the character itself must NOT change style, only
the world around it becomes more cinematic and futuristic.

MOOD: arc from overwhelmed/frantic to calm/in control. Awe-inspiring but
warm, never cold or corporate. The feeling of time being given back, not
technology being intimidating.

AUDIO: starts with a faint fast ticking/whirring sound (multiple clocks),
which fades into a low cinematic swell as the light ripple happens, and
resolves into a single calm, warm ambient tone with one soft satisfying
"chime" when the final clock appears. No dialogue, no music with lyrics.

Duration: 8 seconds.

--no redesign of the character, no extra characters or people, no
readable text, numbers or logos anywhere in frame, no camera shake or
handheld motion, no fast cuts, no glitch/cyberpunk neon aesthetic, no
dystopian or cold mood, no watermark
```
